# Mega / Light-type mod: TODO list and form table

Updated 2026-11-10 (evening), after the "implement all" pass. See "STILL OPEN" at the end. Everything here builds (`make rom`).
Nothing listed below is a crash; these are placeholders, temporary tests and approximations.

## 1. Stand-in abilities

**None left.** Every Mega and Legendary Mega in the spec now has its real ability
(new abilities are IDs 124–180).

The original Mega packs use the abilities your spec gives them. Some of those differ from the
official games, for example Aerodactyl Rock Head instead of Tough Claws and Altaria Cloud Nine
instead of Pixilate. They are deliberate, not stand-ins.

## 2. Placeholder shiny palettes (112)

None of the packs came with real shiny colors. Every `shiny.pal` was generated by
`tools/scripts/import_mega_pack.py` (`make_shiny`), which copies the species' own normal → shiny
color change onto the new form. Hand-made palettes should replace them:

- all 109 Mega / Legendary Mega forms (`res/pokemon/<species>/forms/{mega_1,mega_2,legendary_1}/shiny.pal`)
- `arceus/forms/light`, `dialga/forms/origin`, `palkia/forms/origin`

Edit the JASC `.pal` directly (16 colors, keep index 0). Don't re-run the importer on a species
afterwards, because it overwrites `shiny.pal`. If you have to, use `--skip-existing`.

## 3. Placeholder art

- **Light Arceus** (`arceus/forms/light`): a pale yellow/gold recolor.
- **Mega sprites are scaled up ×1.2** (nearest neighbor) because the packs draw inside 64×64.
  The unscaled originals are in `tools/mega_pack/_unscaled_backup/`.
- **Form party icons** (all 109 Megas, Origin Dialga / Palkia, Light Arceus, Kyurem, Gen 5 forms) are
  generated by `tools/scripts/make_form_icons.py` (shrunk front sprite). Re-run it after adding a form.
- Mega Ring, Legend Necklace, Mega Rock, Mobile PC, DNA Splicers, Reveal Glass and Drive icons are
  installed (`tools/scripts/install_item_icon.py`, `make_gen5_item_icons.py`).

## 4. Temporary tests to remove

**None left.** Lass Sarah's Charizard, Mom's Kyurem / Zekrom / Reshiram gift and the Mega button debug
messages are gone.

## 5. Approximations and known limits

- **Trainer Megas** (from the Coal Badge on) have no AI choice: a trainer's Pokémon always Mega
  Evolves on its first turn spent attacking (not when it switches or uses an item), and goes
  Legendary on a later attacking turn. The Mega Rock a trainer's Pokémon is given exists only in
  that battle's copy of the party.

- **Electric Terrain** has no on-screen indicator, only the start and end messages.
- **Light Plate** has no in-game source.
- **Origin Dialga/Palkia**: no in-battle revert if the Orb is lost mid-battle (it is fixed
  after the battle), and the Pokédex doesn't track Origin forms.
- **Easy chat** only has the original 124 ability words, so the new abilities can't be used there.
- **Trainer AI** doesn't plan around the new abilities. It won't crash, but it ignores them.
- **Mega / Legendary animations** were shortened by about 180 frames each (~3 s), estimated from the
  fade lengths, not timed in-game. The originals are in `tools/mega_pack/_anim_backup/`.
- **Magic Bounce**: in doubles, a move that hits both foes can be bounced only once.
- **Fume Cloud** doesn't affect Rest, held items or other abilities' healing.
- **Time Lock**: "refresh to 3 turns" is read as "at least 3"; it never shortens Trick Room.
- **Seismic Breaker**: the screens break before the damage, but the message shows after the move.
- **Venom Leverage**: a move that poisons two foes lowers Defense on only one of them.
- **Omen Wings** counts only switches from the battle menu (not U-turn or Baton Pass), and the
  boost appears in the checks right after that switch.
- **Emperor Mantle**: the +1 priority is used up by the next damaging Fighting move even if it misses.
- **Third-Eye Curse**: Disable lasts 2 end-of-turns, and won't replace an existing Disable.
- **One-chance abilities**: Silk Swipe and Gilded Claws use their one chance even if the
  target has no item or the Defense drop is blocked.

## Gen 5 species (Phase 2) — loose ends

- **Placeholders:** party icons (shrunk front sprites), footprints copied from a vanilla Pokémon,
  Pokédex entries written for this mod (not the official text), foreign-language names = English,
  body shapes approximate, sprite animations / Pokédex scale copied from a vanilla Pokémon of similar
  height. Gen 5 isn't in the original Pokédex (it is in the Legendary Pokédex).
- **Alternate forms**: all added (see the "Implement all" pass below).
- **Hidden Abilities** are not used (Platinum has none), except Cottonee/Whimsicott (Chlorophyll,
  because neither regular ability exists yet).
- **EXP yield capped at 255** (Platinum limit): Victini, Audino, Hydreigon, all Gen 5 legendaries.
- **Karrablast/Shelmet** evolve by levelling up with the other in the party (not by trade).
- **Meloetta** has no level-1 move until Round exists (Phase 4).
- Per-batch reports: `tools/gen5_pack/import_report_*.txt` (stand-in abilities, skipped moves).

## Legendary Pokédex — finished (2026-11-10)

  **TYPES**, **ABILITIES**. L/R or touch switch tabs; Up/Down move, Left/Right jump a page; B exits.

### DNA Splicers / Kyurem (Phase 8)
- The stored Zekrom / Reshiram lives in the GTS save slot, so only one fusion at a time. While a
  Kyurem is fused, the GTS can't be opened and that Kyurem can't be released from the PC.
- Splicing is refused while you have a Pokémon listed on the GTS.
